Why it stays steady

Outside the 2° deadband, q = clamp((|e|−2)/33, 0, 1); B(q) = 3(1−q)²q(0.10) + 3(1−q)q²(0.90) + q³; turn magnitude 10 + 35B(q). The command accelerates at 300 units/s and decelerates at 1500 units/s. Counterclockwise commands are ×2.5. Left and right are equal and opposite, so mode 2 rotates without translating.

Robust detection

Choose the largest class 3 box by area = w × h, with w ≥ 6 px. A full box updates w_good ← 0.7w_good + 0.3w; a box clipped within 3 px of an image edge borrows that width. On vision loss, follow the remembered IMU target for 2500 ms at no more than 30/127 turn.
